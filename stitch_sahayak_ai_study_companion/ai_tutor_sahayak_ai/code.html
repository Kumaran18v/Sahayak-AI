Based on your uploaded Unit 3 Notes and Operating Systems.pdf, a Deadlock is an impasse where a set of processes are blocked because each process holding a resource is waiting for another resource acquired by some other process.

Coffman Conditions (Required Concurrently) OCR Confidence: 99.4%
  1. 1
    Mutual Exclusion: At least one resource must be held in a non-shareable mode (Unit 3 Notes p.18).
  2. 2
    Hold and Wait: A process is holding at least one resource and requesting additional resources.
  3. 3
    No Preemption: Resources cannot be forcibly preempted; only released voluntarily.
  4. 4
    Circular Wait: A closed chain of processes exists, where each process holds resources needed by the next.
